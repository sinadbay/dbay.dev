What is deekte

The Farsi keyboard that listens

Typing Farsi on iOS is slow. deekte (دیکته, "dictation") is a custom Persian keyboard you can use in any app — plus a one-tap voice dictation feature. Tap the mic, speak Persian, and your words are transcribed and inserted straight into whatever you're writing: Messages, Notes, WhatsApp, Telegram, and more.

Speech recognition is powered by AI tuned for Farsi, so dictation is fast and accurate. No account, no sign-up — just add the keyboard and start talking.

Privacy

Anonymous by design

deekte has no account and no sign-up. We don't log your keystrokes or collect the text you type. Audio is used only while you're actively dictating, streamed over an encrypted connection for transcription, and is never stored on our servers.
